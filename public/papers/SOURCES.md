## Fast delivery of heralded atom–photon quantum correlation

- **File:** `fast-delivery-2024-fig1.webp` — 999 × 755 pixels, 104,210 bytes.
- **Publication key:** `zhang_12km_atom_photon`.
- **Attribution:** Sheng Zhang, Jixuan Shi, Yibo Liang, Yuedong Sun, Yukai Wu, Luming Duan and Yunfei Pu, “Fast delivery of heralded atom-photon quantum correlation over 12 km fiber through multiplexing enhancement,” *Nature Communications* **15**, 10306 (2024), Figure 1.
- **Original figure title:** “Experimental setup and scheme.”
- **What it shows:** The proposed repeater context and the experiment's multiplexed atom–photon correlation protocol, including outgoing signal photons and the heralding return path. The larger repeater network is conceptual; this experiment implements one side of an elementary link using fibers in the laboratory.
- **Source:** [Article and full caption](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-54691-3) · [Figure 1](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-54691-3/figures/1) · [Original PNG](https://media.springernature.com/full/springer-static/image/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41467-024-54691-3/MediaObjects/41467_2024_54691_Fig1_HTML.png).

## CNN–BiLSTM entanglement classification

- **File:** `cnn-bilstm-2026-fig1.webp` — 747 × 446 pixels, 79,808 bytes.
- **Publication key:** `sun_sample_efficient_entanglement`.
- **Attribution:** Qian Sun, Yuedong Sun, Yu Hu, Yihan Ma, Runqi Han and Nan Jiang, “Towards Sample-Efficient Entanglement Classification for 3–4 Qubit Systems: A Tailored CNN-BiLSTM Approach,” arXiv:2601.22562v1 (2026), Figure 1.
- **Original caption opening:** “The data transmission process of the two CNN-BiLSTM architectures.”
