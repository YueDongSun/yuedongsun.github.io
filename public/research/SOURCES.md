## CNN–BiLSTM architecture and benchmark figures

Attribution: Qian Sun, Yuedong Sun, Yu Hu, Yihan Ma, Runqi Han and Nan Jiang, “Towards Sample-Efficient Entanglement Classification for 3–4 Qubit Systems: A Tailored CNN-BiLSTM Approach,” arXiv:2601.22562v1 (2026). The arXiv HTML version identifies [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) as its licence.

- `cnn_bilstm_architectures.webp`: complete Fig. 1, copied from the site's existing lossless publication asset. [Figure and caption](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.22562v1#S3.F1).
- `cnn_bilstm_benchmarks.svg`: complete original Fig. 5, retained byte-for-byte from [the arXiv SVG](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.22562v1/fig5.svg). It compares model classification accuracy across training-set sizes for 3- and 4-qubit systems. [Figure and caption](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.22562v1#S4.F5).
- `cnn_bilstm_benchmarks.webp`: 1600 × 778 web rendering of the full Fig. 5 SVG on white. The original SVG remains available in the enlarged viewer.

These images are labelled as preprint figures.
